## IELTS 8.0

### 5 words / collocations
1. **bolster** (v) – củng cố, tăng cường. *Subsidies can bolster small businesses.* Lỗi: dùng với thứ tiêu cực. Thay cho "make stronger".
2. **detrimental to** – có hại cho. *Constant notifications are detrimental to concentration.* Lỗi: "detrimental for". Thay cho "bad for".
3. **tangible benefits** – lợi ích cụ thể, thấy được. *Vocational training brings tangible benefits for school leavers.* Lỗi: "tangible" không dùng cho cảm xúc.
4. **stem from** – bắt nguồn từ. *Many mistakes stem from a lack of experience.* Lỗi: "stem from to".
5. **a growing body of evidence** – ngày càng nhiều bằng chứng. *A growing body of evidence suggests that sleep improves memory.* Lỗi: "a growing evidence" (evidence không đếm được).

### Grammar for Writing: Cleft sentence (câu nhấn mạnh)
- Form: **It is/was + phần cần nhấn + that/who ...** hoặc **What + S + V + is ...**
- Example: *It is early education that shapes a child's attitude to learning.* / *What matters most is practical experience.*
- Task 2: dùng ở topic sentence hoặc phần nêu quan điểm để nhấn ý chính. Đừng dùng quá 2 lần.

### MODEL ANSWER (đề hôm qua/gần nhất: public transport vs new roads — Agree/Disagree)
Governments face a choice between expanding road networks and upgrading public transport. I largely agree that the latter deserves priority.

Firstly, new roads rarely solve congestion. Wider highways tend to attract more cars, so traffic soon returns to its former level. By contrast, a well-run metro moves far more passengers per hour while taking up much less space. Were governments to invest in rail, commuting times would fall sharply and cities would become cleaner, since fewer private vehicles would be on the road.

Secondly, public transport promotes social fairness. Not everyone can afford a car, and elderly or low-income residents depend on buses and trains to reach jobs and hospitals. Reliable services therefore widen opportunity in a way that motorways cannot.

Admittedly, roads remain essential in rural areas, where low population density makes rail uneconomical, and freight still relies heavily on lorries. However, this argues for balanced spending rather than for putting roads first.

In conclusion, investing in public transport offers greater long-term benefits for the economy, the environment and equality, so it should take priority.

**Why it scores (3 dòng):**
1. Task Response: nêu rõ quan điểm ở mở bài, mỗi đoạn một ý chính có giải thích, có nhượng bộ (Admittedly) rồi quay lại lập trường.
2. Cohesion: dùng linking tự nhiên (Firstly, By contrast, However) và đại từ/paraphrase, không máy móc.
3. Range: đảo ngữ điều kiện (Were governments to...), từ vựng chính xác, câu phức đa dạng và gần như không lỗi.

## Everyday English

### Scenario: Phone call — rescheduling a delivery
- Agent: Hello, FastShip customer service. How can I help?
- You: Hi, I'm calling about a parcel due today. Could I reschedule it?
- Agent: Sure. Can I have the tracking number?
- You: It's VN4471. Sorry, bear with me, I need to find it.
- Agent: No worries, take your time.
- You: Okay, got it. I won't be home until six. Can you deliver it tomorrow instead?
- Agent: Tomorrow morning works. Is anyone available to sign?
- You: Yes, my flatmate will be in. Could you leave it with the front desk otherwise?
- Agent: Absolutely, I've noted that. You'll get a text confirmation.
- You: Perfect, thanks. Sorry, the line's breaking up. Could you say that again?

### Say it like a native
| Textbook | More natural |
|---|---|
| Please wait a moment. | Bear with me / Hold on a sec. |
| I will call again. | I'll try again later. |
| The signal is bad. | You're breaking up. |
| I want to change the time. | Can I move it to Friday? |
| Can you repeat? | Sorry, could you say that again? |

### 2 idioms / phrasal verbs
- **bear with me** – xin chờ tôi một chút. *Bear with me while I check the system.*
- **get through (to someone)** – gọi được/liên lạc được. *I couldn't get through to the helpline all morning.*

### Shadowing
1. "Bear with me for a second." – nối "with me" /wɪð-mi/; nhấn **bear**, **second**.
2. "I'll try again later." – "I'll" nhẹ, nối "try a-gain"; nhấn **try**, **later**.
3. "Could you say that again?" – "Could you" → /kʊ-dʒə/; nhấn **say**, **again**.
