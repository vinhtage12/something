2. Rewrite using negative inversion: "Remote work saves time. It also reduces emissions."
3. "Mitigate" or "exacerbate": which word fits "___ the impact of pollution"? Which one makes things worse?
4. Spot the error: "Social media is a double-edged sword to teenagers" and "Widespread adoption of remote work have reshaped cities."

4. "for teenagers" (không phải "to"); "adoption ... has reshaped" (chủ ngữ số ít: adoption).

## IELTS 8.0

### 5 words / collocations
1. **curb** (v) – kiềm chế, hạn chế. *Higher taxes could curb car use in city centres.* Lỗi: dùng "curb" cho thứ tốt (curb growth of happiness). Paraphrase: reduce → curb / rein in.
2. **pervasive** (adj) – lan tràn khắp nơi. *Advertising is pervasive in modern life.* Lỗi: nhầm với "persuasive". Tránh lặp "very common".
3. **a steep learning curve** – giai đoạn học rất khó lúc đầu. *Migrating to a new system involves a steep learning curve.* Lỗi: "high learning curve".
4. **foster** (v) – nuôi dưỡng, thúc đẩy. *Public parks foster a sense of community.* Thay cho "help to develop / encourage".
5. **far-reaching consequences** – hệ quả sâu rộng. *Cutting funding for schools has far-reaching consequences.* Lỗi: "far-reach".

### Grammar for Writing: Conditional inversion (không dùng "if")
- Form: **Should + S + V...** / **Were + S + to V...** / **Had + S + V3...**, S + would/could + V
- Example: *Were governments to invest in rail, congestion would fall sharply.* / *Should funding be cut, services would deteriorate.*
- Task 2: dùng 1 lần ở đoạn giải pháp hoặc dự đoán hệ quả để thể hiện range ngữ pháp. Không dùng "if" + đảo ngữ cùng lúc.

### Model answer for yesterday's prompt (Speaking Part 2: technology that changed the way you work)
"I'd like to talk about my code editor with an AI assistant built in, which I started using about two years ago. Before that, I'd spend hours searching for answers online. Now I simply describe the problem, and it suggests a solution I can adapt. Not only does it save me time, but it also helps me spot bugs I would otherwise overlook. Admittedly, there was a steep learning curve at first, because I had to learn how to phrase my requests precisely. All in all, it has transformed my routine: I spend less time on repetitive tasks and more on design decisions, which is the part of my job I enjoy most."

**Why it scores (3 dòng):**
1. Bám đủ 4 ý của cue card theo thứ tự, mạch lạc, không lạc đề.
2. Từ vựng tự nhiên, chính xác (steep learning curve, overlook, repetitive tasks) và 1 cấu trúc đảo ngữ.
3. Có ý kiến cân bằng (Admittedly...) và kết bằng ý chính → fluency + coherence cao.

## Everyday English

### Scenario: Ordering at a coffee shop
- Barista: Hi there! What can I get you?
- You: Hi, could I get a medium oat latte, please?
- Barista: Sure. Hot or iced?
- You: Iced, please. And is that blueberry muffin fresh?
- Barista: Baked this morning. Want one?
- You: Yes, please. Can I get that to go?
- Barista: No problem. That's 8.50. Name for the order?
- You: Vinh. Oh, could I get an extra shot in the latte?
- Barista: Of course, that one's on the house.
- You: Thanks, that's really kind of you!

### Say it like a native
| Textbook | More natural |
|---|---|
| I want a coffee. | Could I get a coffee? / I'll have a coffee. |
| Take away, please. | To go, please. |
| How much is it? | What's the damage? (đùa vui) / What do I owe you? |
| Please add more sugar. | Could I get a bit more sugar on the side? |
| Thank you very much. | Cheers, I appreciate it. |

### 2 idioms / phrasal verbs
- **on the house** – miễn phí do quán tặng. *The dessert was on the house.*
- **stop by** – ghé qua. *I'll stop by the café on my way to work.*

### Shadowing
1. "Could I get a latte to go?" – nối "get a" → /ge-tə/; "to go" nhấn **go**.
2. "That one's on the house." – nối "on the" → /ɒn-ðə/; nhấn **house**.
3. "I'll stop by later." – "stop by" nối /stɒp-baɪ/; nhấn **stop**, **later**.
