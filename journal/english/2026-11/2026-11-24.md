## IELTS 8.0

### 5 words / collocations
1. **mitigate** (v) – làm giảm nhẹ tác hại. *Governments should mitigate the impact of urban pollution.* Lỗi hay gặp: dùng "mitigate" cho thứ tốt. Paraphrase: reduce → alleviate / mitigate.
2. **a double-edged sword** – con dao hai lưỡi. *Social media is a double-edged sword for teenagers.* Dùng làm câu mở cho bài "advantages/disadvantages".
3. **exacerbate** (v) – làm trầm trọng hơn. *Long commutes exacerbate stress.* Đừng nói "exacerbate the benefits".
4. **widespread adoption** – sự áp dụng rộng rãi. *The widespread adoption of remote work has reshaped cities.* Tránh lặp "many people use".
5. **take a toll on** – gây tổn hại dần dần. *Sleep deprivation takes a toll on productivity.* Lỗi: "take a toll to".

### Grammar for Writing: Negative inversion
- Form: **Not only + auxiliary + subject + verb, but (also) ...**
- Example: *Not only does remote work save time, but it also reduces emissions.*
- Task 2: dùng 1 lần trong body paragraph để nhấn mạnh 2 lợi ích. Nhớ đảo trợ động từ (does/do/did); đừng lạm dụng quá 1–2 lần.

## Everyday English

### Scenario: Daily standup meeting
**Dialogue**
- Lead: Morning, everyone. Let's keep it quick. Linh, want to kick us off?
- Linh: Sure. Yesterday I wrapped up the login bug. Today I'm picking up the payment API.
- Lead: Any blockers?
- Linh: One. I'm waiting on the staging environment, and it's been down since Monday.
- Lead: Okay, I'll chase that up with DevOps. Minh, how about you?
- Minh: I hit a snag with the database migration. It fails on large tables.
- Lead: Could you loop in Hà? She dealt with something similar last month.
- Minh: Good idea. I'll ping her right after this.
- Lead: Great. Let's circle back at the end of the day if it's still stuck.
- Minh: Sounds good.

### Say it like a native
| Textbook | More natural |
|---|---|
| I have finished it. | I've wrapped it up. |
| I have a problem. | I've hit a snag. |
| Please tell me later. | Can you get back to me on that? |
| I don't understand. | I'm not following. Could you walk me through it? |
| I am busy now. | I'm swamped at the moment. |

### 2 idioms / phrasal verbs
- **loop (someone) in** – đưa ai vào cuộc trao đổi. *Please loop me in on that email thread.*
- **circle back** – quay lại vấn đề sau. *Let's circle back to pricing next week.* (phổ biến trong môi trường công sở, hơi "corporate")

### Shadowing (nghe/nhại lại, nối âm)
1. "I've hit a snag with the migration." – nối "hit a" → /hɪ-tə/; nhấn **snag**, **migration**.
2. "Could you loop him in?" – "loop him in" nối thành /luːp-pɪm-in/; nhấn **loop**.
3. "Let's circle back later." – "Let's" nuốt âm t nhẹ; nhấn **circle**, **later**.
