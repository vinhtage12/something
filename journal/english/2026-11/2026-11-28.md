## IELTS 8.0

### 5 words / collocations
1. **alleviate** (v) – làm dịu, giảm bớt (khó khăn, đau đớn). *New bus lanes could alleviate traffic congestion.* Lỗi: dùng với thứ tích cực. Paraphrase: reduce → alleviate / ease.
2. **bolster** (v) – củng cố, tăng cường. *Subsidies can bolster the local economy.* Lỗi: "bolster up" thừa; dùng "bolster" + tân ngữ trực tiếp.
3. **a tangible impact** – tác động hữu hình, rõ rệt. *The scheme has had a tangible impact on youth employment.* Lỗi: "tangible" không đi với thứ trừu tượng thuần như "tangible idea". Tránh lặp "a clear effect".
4. **disproportionately affect** – ảnh hưởng không cân xứng. *Rising rents disproportionately affect low-income families.* Lỗi: viết "disproportionally" (ít dùng hơn, vẫn đúng nhưng "disproportionately" phổ biến hơn).
5. **hinge on** (phr. v) – phụ thuộc vào. *The success of the policy hinges on public support.* Lỗi: "hinge in/of". Thay cho "depend on".

### Grammar for Writing: Cleft sentence (câu nhấn mạnh)
- Form: **What + S + V + is/was + ...** / **It is/was + X + that ...**
- Example: *What many employers overlook is the value of rest.* / *It is education, not wealth, that shapes long-term opportunity.*
- Task 2: dùng ở đầu đoạn thân bài hoặc câu kết luận để nhấn mạnh ý chính. Đừng dùng quá 1–2 lần/bài.

### Model answer for the last prompt (Writing Task 2, Agree/Disagree: public transport vs new roads)
It is widely argued that governments should invest in public transport rather than build new roads. I largely agree, although roads still have a role in some regions.

The main advantage of public transport is its capacity to alleviate congestion. A single train can carry as many passengers as hundreds of cars, so a well-funded network takes vehicles off the road and shortens journeys for everyone. By contrast, new roads often attract extra traffic, which means the relief they bring is short-lived. Moreover, cheaper and more reliable transport disproportionately benefits low-income residents, who cannot afford to run a car.

Environmental concerns point the same way. Were authorities to expand rail and bus services, emissions would fall, and cities would become healthier places to live. What makes this approach persuasive is that it bolsters economic activity too, since workers can reach jobs more easily.

Nevertheless, in remote areas with low population density, rail may be impractical, and well-maintained roads remain essential. Ultimately, the success of any policy hinges on matching investment to local needs.

In conclusion, public transport deserves priority, though roads should not be neglected entirely.

**Why it scores (3 dòng):**
1. Task Response: nêu rõ quan điểm, có nhượng bộ (remote areas) và kết luận nhất quán.
2. Coherence: mỗi đoạn một ý chính, dùng linking tự nhiên (By contrast, Moreover, Nevertheless).
3. Lexis/Grammar: collocation chính xác, đảo ngữ điều kiện (Were authorities to...) và câu nhấn mạnh (What makes this...).

## Everyday English

### Scenario: Phone call to reschedule an appointment
- Receptionist: Good morning, Greenfield Dental. How can I help?
- You: Hi, I have an appointment on Friday at ten, but something's come up. Is it possible to reschedule?
- Receptionist: Let me check the diary. Would early next week suit you?
- You: Tuesday would be ideal, if you have anything in the afternoon.
- Receptionist: I can fit you in at half past two.
- You: That works for me. Could you put me down for that?
- Receptionist: Done. Can I take your name and number again?
- You: Sure, it's Vinh, and the number is 0912 345 678.
- Receptionist: Lovely. We'll send a reminder text the day before.
- You: Perfect, thanks for being so flexible. Bye now!

### Say it like a native
| Textbook | More natural |
|---|---|
| I want to change my appointment. | Could I move my appointment? |
| Please wait a moment. | Bear with me a second. / Hold on a sec. |
| I will call you again. | I'll ring you back. (Anh-Anh) / I'll call you back. |
| Is this time good for you? | Does that work for you? |
| I am sorry for the trouble. | Sorry for the hassle. |

### 2 idioms / phrasal verbs
- **fit (someone) in** – sắp xếp được lịch cho ai. *The doctor can fit you in this afternoon.*
- **play it by ear** – tuỳ cơ ứng biến, chưa lên kế hoạch cụ thể. *I'm not sure about the weekend, so let's play it by ear.*

### Shadowing
1. "Something's come up." – nối "come up" → /kʌm-ʌp/; nhấn **come**; "something's" đọc gọn /ˈsʌmθɪŋz/.
2. "Could you put me down for that?" – "put me" nối /pʊt-mi/; nhấn **put**, **down**.
3. "Bear with me a second." – "with me" nối nhẹ; nhấn **bear**, **second**.
